Standards

Editorial Guidelines

Vice City Feed is an independent desk. These rules are the product.

Affiliation

We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, or any official Grand Theft Auto channel. Trademarks belong to their owners. Coverage is commentary, criticism, and player reporting.

Leaks and rumors

Trailer analysis is labeled as analysis. Unverified dumps are not republished. We do not pay for stolen assets or personal data. If a claim cannot be sourced, it stays off the Wire or sits in the rumor bin with that label.

Spoilers

Mission Intel and character studies may spoil story beats. In-article spoilers use a reveal control. Headlines should not blow a twist when a warning will do.

Corrections

If we get map facts, leak status, or attribution wrong, we update the piece and note the change. Email the desk via the newsletter form with "Correction" in the body.

Community

Discord and X are distribution, not sourcing. A viral clip is not a citation. Be kind in the comments you control; we will not host harassment.
