The Keys, the Swamps, and the Stories Buried in Leonida

South of Vice City's last condo, Leonida stops performing. The Keys and the Grassrivers are where the game hides its folklore: handmade signs, bait-shop radio, and wildlife that will wreck a getaway you planned on asphalt.

The swamp is a stealth map

Grassrivers at night is not "the rural part." It is a visibility puzzle. Headlights are a flare. Moonlight on water is a trail.

Pro Tip

Cut your lights ten seconds before a water crossing. The reflection gives you away to NPC boats before the engine does.

Some of the best writing in GTA VI is a handwritten note on a fridge in a house you only entered to steal a truck.

The Keys are a logistics puzzle

Fuel, weather, and ferry timing. A pretty screenshot of a causeway is not the same as crossing it in a storm with a wanted level.

Spoiler

Wildlife is not flavor text

Alligators, birds that lift at the wrong time, and a boar that will end a stealth approach. Treat animals as extra NPCs with worse pathfinding and better teeth.

Drive slow. Look at the signs. The state is the story when the city is asleep.
