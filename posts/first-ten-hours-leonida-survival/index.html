GUIDESFirst 10 Hours in Leonida: Banks, Bikes, and Not Getting Wasted

Your first ten hours in Leonida will try to kill your save with optimism: too many guns, too much highway, not enough stash.

This is the opener we wish we had.

Stash before style

Buy the ugly apartment you can afford. Put cash in it. The neon penthouse is a trailer, not a plan.

Pro Tip

Do the Pink Sands Getaway before you buy a loud car. You want a bike that can abandon a causeway, not a coupe that loves guardrails.

Cop patterns that end new players

  1. Checkpoint patience — they wait now.
  2. Bike dismounts — assume the cop survives the crash.
  3. County handoff — leaving Vice City is not an escape hatch.

The map is bigger. That does not mean you are safer. It means you can die farther from your bed.

Banks, then bikes, then missions

Do not mainline story until you can lose one star without a YouTube tab. Port Gellhorn will humble you if the sandbox hasn't yet.

Spoiler

Survive the opener. The city gets weirder after dark, and you will want the save file.
