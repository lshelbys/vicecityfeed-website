Bayside Marina is where the garage vertical goes to sea. Lift kits on boats sound like a meme until you see neon undertrays survive salt spray in the rain.

This is a preview of a space that wants to be a community hub.

## Boats as garage citizens

Hull liveries, interior kits, and a parts cage that looks one folder away from a PC pack.

:::protip
Store a civilian tender and a loud toy. The marina security reads chrome faster than Vice PD reads underglow.
:::
