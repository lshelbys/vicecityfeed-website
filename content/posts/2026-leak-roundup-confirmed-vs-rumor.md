Leaks are a weather system around GTA VI. This is our quarterly ledger: what we treat as confirmed, what is plausible, and what you should stop forwarding in group chats.

Vice City Feed does not launder DAT Miners Anonymous as journalism. We also do not pretend trailers are novels.

## Confirmed — or close enough

Rockstar's own footage, ratings boards, and official captions lock in the dual protagonists, Leonida as a Florida-shaped state, Vice City as the urban core, and a wanted/police loop that includes helicopters *and* watercraft.

Map variety — beaches, swamps, Keys-like islands — is no longer rumor. It is the trailer language.

> If a claim only exists as a cropped screenshot with no provenance, it is not "intel." It is content.

## Plausible, not proven

- A more granular ammo/crafting bench than GTA V.
- Property that functions as small businesses, not just interiors.
- Weather cells that alter driving and wanted escapes.
- A PC mod pipeline that is less hostile than the last generation.

Each of those is consistent with systems we can already see. None of them deserve "LEAKED GAMEPLAY" thumbnails.

:::protip
When a clip has no HUD, no pause, and a color grade that looks like a music video, search for the original audio. A surprising number of 2026 "leaks" are edited from the same 90 seconds of official footage.
:::

## The rumor bin

Map-size numbers with fake precision, celebrity cameo lists, and "Lucia can date NPCs in a full romance sim" posts are fan fiction until they are not. We will update this page. We will not chase every Discord screenshot.

:::spoiler
One widely shared "north Leonida snow biome" still is a color-graded fog take. There is no credible evidence of a winter region. If that changes, it will be on the Wire with a correction, not a victory lap.
:::

## How this desk handles leaks

Read the [editorial guidelines](/editorial-guidelines). We do not pay for dumps. We do not republish personal data. We do attribute trailer analysis as analysis.
