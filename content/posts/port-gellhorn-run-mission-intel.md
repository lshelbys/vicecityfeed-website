The Port Gellhorn Run is the first Heist that feels like GTA VI instead of a prettier GTA V. Dual roles, a 22-minute timer, and a crane that punishes anyone who treats stealth as optional.

This is Mission Intel, not a checkbox wiki. Loadout, split, and the three fail states that actually end the job.

## Recommended split

- **Lucia** — catwalk overwatch, suppressed rifle, eyes on the office windows.
- **Jason** — dock level, cutter, quiet until the container drop.

If you reverse this, you can still finish. You will spend more of the timer in gunfire.

## The timer is teaching you the map

22:00 is generous if you do not loot the side warehouse. It is tight if you do. That is the design: greed versus the ferry.

> The boat does not wait because the story is dramatic. It waits because the state has a schedule.

:::protip
Ping the camera on the eastern mast before you cut the first fence. If it stays live, Lucia's overwatch is wasted on the wrong cone and Jason eats a two-star from dock security.
:::

## Loadout

Bring a suppressed sidearm, a long gun with a stable optic, and **no** underglow on the approach car. Port floodlights love neon.

Ammo: hollow points for office interiors, standard for the yard. If you have slugs, save them; you should not be in a shotgun argument here.

:::spoiler
If Jason goes down after the container drop, do not restart. Switch to Lucia, skip the loot room, and take the west ladder straight to the skiff. The Heist still cash-completes at a lower take. The game counts it as a "clean exit."
:::

## Fail states worth knowing

1. **Crane spotlight** — standing up on the catwalk during the container move.
2. **Office printer** — an NPC call you can intercept if you are already inside.
3. **Causeway patrol** — after the boat, a county unit that is not in the briefing.

Walk it twice on Street-adjacent saves before you chase a Legend time. The map is the puzzle. The guns are the punctuation.
