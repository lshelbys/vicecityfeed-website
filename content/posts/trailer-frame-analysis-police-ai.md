We do not treat trailers as leaks. We treat them as edited sentences. Slowed down, the latest GTA VI footage still gives up storefronts, a two-stage storm, and police behavior that is not GTA V with extra chrome.

## Businesses that look enterable

Several signs that looked like textures in motion have interior lighting that reacts to the storm. That is a tell. Rockstar does not usually waste dynamic light on cardboard.

:::media caption="Frame 1,204: a pawn-shop interior light pops a half-second after lightning. That is a room, not a decal." accent="cyan"
:::

## Weather as a wanted tool

The storm is not just pretty. In one chase, a helicopter peels off when the cell thickens. In GTA V that would be a scripted beat. Here it looks like a rule: visibility taxes air units.

> If weather can fire your helicopter, then weather is a stealth mechanic.

:::protip
Watch tire spray, not the skybox. Spray direction in two shots implies wind shifting 90 degrees — a cell, not a looping rain texture.
:::

## Police AI tells

Units hold corners longer. A bike cop dismounts instead of suiciding into a wall. Backup arrives from a parallel street, not from a magic alley.

:::spoiler
A brief radio bark in the mix uses a county callsign, not Vice PD. If that survives into the build, wanted levels can hand you off across jurisdictions — which matches what we are seeing on the map desk.
:::
