The last generation of GTA Online was a walled garden that the PC community still somehow turned into a fashion show. Vice City's vehicle pipeline looks — cautiously — more hospitable.

This is not a promise from Rockstar. It is a reading of the garage, the livery stack, and what trailer UI implies about layers.

## What we want on day one

- Layered livery export
- Honest model names
- Lighting presets that match rain/night
- A script hook that does not nuke Story Mode

:::media caption="If the PC toolchain matches the in-game layer stack, garage culture wins." accent="cyan"
:::

## What we expect instead

A delayed official tool. Fantastic five-person teams shipping packs anyway. A first month of broken replacements. Then a house style: Vice City neon, wet asphalt, pastel crime.

:::protip
If you are archiving, save uncompressed TGA/PNG sources, not just in-game previews. The in-engine viewer lies about pearl coats.
:::

> Modders will finish the city Rockstar starts. That has been true since 2002. The question is how much they have to fight the format.

:::spoiler
Several vehicle nodes in trailer UI use names that match a more granular kit system than GTA V's. If those names survive to PC, replacement mods get a lot less destructive.
:::

Until then, build in-game. The garage is already a statement.
