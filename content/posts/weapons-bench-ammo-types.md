GTA VI's gunplay is still Rockstar-punchy. The new language is **ammo types** and a weapons bench that lets you craft a fight instead of just upgrading a bar.

## The bench is a kitchen

You are not filling a meter. You are cooking a loadout for a district. Vice City interiors hate overpenetration. Grassrivers loves slugs. The Keys, in wind, punish anything without a stable optic.

:::media caption="Weapons bench lighting is intentionally ugly. You are meant to look at the brass, not the skyline." accent="magenta"
:::

## Ammo as dialect

- **Hollow point** — hotels, offices, club back rooms.
- **Standard FMJ** — yards, highways, anything with cars in the way.
- **Slug / buck** — close swamp work, not the marina cocktail set.
- **Tracer** — looks cool, tells every cop where you live.

> If you are still buying one ammo type for every gun, you are playing an older game inside this one.

:::protip
Craft on downtime in the garage, not in a restroom stall mid-wanted. The bench animation is long on purpose. It is telling you to plan.
:::
